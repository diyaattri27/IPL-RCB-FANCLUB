About Royal Challengers Bangaluru

Royal Challengers Bengaluru, formerly known as Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB), are a professional Twenty20 cricket team based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, competing in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Founded in 2008 by United Spirits, the team’s home ground is the iconic M. Chinnaswamy Stadium.

RCB won their maiden IPL title in 2025 under the captaincy of Rajat Patidar, with the team finishing as runners-up in 2009, 2011, and 2016. Over the years, the franchise has seen legendary players like Virat Kohli, AB de Villiers, and Glenn Maxwell wear the red and gold. The team has also qualified for playoffs in 10 out of 18 seasons, establishing a reputation for thrilling cricket.

RCB has participated in the Champions League T20, finishing as runners-up in 2011. The franchise has strong partnerships with major brands, including Qatar Airways, Jio, KEI Wires & Cables, and Birla Estates. As of 2024, RCB was valued at $117 million, making it one of the most valuable IPL teams. The team’s fan base, known as the “RCBians,” is one of the most passionate in the league, supporting every match with unmatched enthusiasm. 🔴🟡
